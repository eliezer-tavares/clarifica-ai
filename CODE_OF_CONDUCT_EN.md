# Code of Conduct — Clarifica.ai

> Em português: [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our pledge

As members, contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This pledge carries extra weight in this project: Clarifica.ai exists for disabled and neurodivergent people — and accessibility applies to the discussions too. Here that means plain language, descriptions of shared images, patience with screen readers and respect for the different ways people interact.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different experiences, viewpoints and ways of communicating (including longer or less formal replies from people using assistive technologies).
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully and offer it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for the project's users.
- Describe images and screenshots when sharing them in issues and pull requests, so blind and low-vision people can follow everything.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwanted attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information (address, e-mail and the like) without explicit consent.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and taking fair corrective action in response to any unacceptable behavior, including removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, edits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, communicating the reasons for the decision when appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies when the project is being represented: in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), in official online spaces, at events and by e-mail. It also applies outside these spaces when a project representative acts in that role.

## Enforcement

Abusive, hostile or unacceptable behavior can be reported to the maintainer by e-mail (contact@eliezertavaresdeoliveira.com). All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with confidentiality and respect for the reporter; the identity is not disclosed without consent. Good-faith reporting is never met with retaliation.

Enforcement is graduated in four levels: **1. Correction** (a private warning about the infraction and why it is inappropriate); **2. Warning** (consequences for repeated behavior, defined in writing); **3. Temporary ban** (no community interaction for a defined period); **4. Permanent ban** (for a continuous pattern of infractions, harassment or aggression against individuals or groups). Maintainers follow these levels and may skip steps in severe cases.
